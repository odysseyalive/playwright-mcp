The Tide Tables of Cascadia

Along the Cascadia margin, the semidiurnal tide swings through a range that few other temperate coastlines match. Field crews working the outer estuaries rely on printed tables that predate satellite altimetry, and the discrepancies between the old almanacs and modern gauges tell a story about a coast that is still, slowly, rising and falling with the land beneath it.

The reference gauges at Astoria and Neah Bay anchor the regional datum. Their century-long records remain the backbone of every working tide table on the shelf, and the longer they run the more they reveal about secular drift.
