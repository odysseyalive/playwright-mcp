Notes on Salt Marsh Recovery

By Devon Asher

November 2, 2025

Restored salt marshes accrete sediment at rates that surprise the engineers who design them. Within a decade of tidal reconnection, a leveed former pasture can build enough elevation to keep pace with local sea-level rise, provided the sediment supply holds and the channels are allowed to wander.

The slow part is biological. Ribbed mussels and cordgrass arrive on their own schedule, and the marsh does not behave like a finished landscape until the invertebrate community has filled in beneath the visible vegetation.
